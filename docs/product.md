# AEGIS INVEST — Product Specification & Philosophy

## 1. Product Identity & Purpose

**AEGIS INVEST** is an institutional-grade, AI-assisted, risk-aware investment decision-intelligence platform. It is engineered to bridge quantitative analytics, fundamental valuation, market regime detection, macroeconomic context, and machine learning into a coherent decision-support workspace.

### Core Philosophy

```text
DATA → ANALYSIS → RISK → EVIDENCE → DECISION INTELLIGENCE
```

Aegis Invest operates on strict epistemological foundations:
- **No Hallucinated Data**: Missing records are explicitly flagged as unknown rather than interpolated or filled with synthetic fiction.
- **Probabilistic Reality**: Financial markets are fundamentally non-stationary and stochastic. The platform never promises guaranteed alpha, fixed risk minimums, or deterministic forecasts.
- **Auditability**: Every indicator, valuation model output, and decision recommendation must produce an auditable provenance trail back to point-in-time source data.

## 2. Decision Questions Addressed

1. **What is happening in the market?** Multi-asset performance, market breadth, volatility structure, and factor rotations.
2. **What is happening to this company?** Operating performance, balance sheet health, cash flow quality, and consensus variance.
3. **Why is it happening?** Earnings revisions, macroeconomic headwinds, margin compression, or sentiment shifts.
4. **What does the empirical evidence support?** Backtested strategy efficacy, factor significance, and historical regime similarities.
5. **What could go wrong?** Tail risk analysis, stress scenarios, drawdown vulnerability, and correlation breakdowns.

## 3. Product Roles & Target Users

- **Portfolio Managers**: Multi-asset asset allocation, risk-budgeting, scenario analysis, and factor constraint enforcement.
- **Quantitative Researchers**: Factor exploration, backtesting engine, strategy tournament execution, and execution modeling.
- **Fundamental Equity Analysts**: Valuation modeling (DCF, multiples), earnings transcript analysis, and financial statement scrutiny.
- **Risk Officers**: Independent VaR/CVaR monitoring, stress test generation, and leverage boundary oversight.
